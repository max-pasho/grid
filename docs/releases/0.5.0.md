GRID 0.5.0 adds a startup animation and animated menus, an icon grid for Files, and a Now Playing widget that can float above every page. If you're on 0.4.x, replace GRID.app with this one; your settings, layout, widgets and browsing data carry over.

![Files as an icon grid, with the Now Playing widget floating above it](https://github.com/max-pasho/grid/blob/v0.5.0/docs/screenshots/files-grid.png?raw=true)

## Install
1. Download **GRID-0.5.0-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **GRID.app** to Applications (replace the old one if asked).
2. This build isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open it once, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (you only need to do this once).
3. Optional: check the download against **GRID-0.5.0-macOS.zip.sha256** with `shasum -a 256 GRID-0.5.0-macOS.zip`.

**Requires macOS 15** (browser extensions need macOS 15.4). Built for Apple silicon.

## What's new

### Motion
- **Startup build-on:** when the window opens, its frame traces in from the top-left corner, the bar rules extend, a scan line passes down the window, and then the bars and panels build on. It takes under a second and never holds up typing. Turn it off in **Settings › Effects**.
- **Menus unfold:** right-click and ⋯ menus now draw a bright line along the edge nearest the pointer and unfold from it, with the title typing on. When they close they fold back into the line, like an old monitor switching off. Clicks work from the first frame.
- Both are skipped when Motion is off or macOS Reduce Motion is on.

### Files icon grid
- The new ▦ button in the Files header shows a folder as an icon grid instead of a list.
- The icons are drawn by GRID: folders with a front flap and badges on well-known folders (home, Desktop, Downloads, Pictures, `.git`, `node_modules` and others), and pages showing their extension (SWIFT, PNG, ZIP…). Executables are amber, app bundles are octagons, links get a link badge and an underlined name, and broken links are dashed. Hidden items are dimmed.
- When a folder opens, the icons trace and fill one after another. A ".." cell takes you up a level, and the arrow keys move by cell and by row. Selection, drag and drop, the right-click menu, rename and Quick Look all work as in the list.

### Now Playing widget
- Add **Now Playing** from the deck's + gallery to see what **Apple Music** or **Spotify** is playing, with elapsed time and previous / play-pause / next buttons.
- **Cover art in GRID's colours:** artwork is redrawn as a dot matrix or as scan lines in your theme's colour, or hidden. It scans in when the track changes.
- **The track shows in the status bar** on every page, with a play/pause button.
- **Track changes need no permission.** The controls and artwork need macOS **Automation** permission for that player. GRID asks only when you press **Connect** or a control, and it never opens Music or Spotify itself.

### Floating widgets
- **Pop any built-in widget out** (the new button on each card, or its ⋯ menu) into a small window that stays on screen whichever page you're on. Its pin button keeps it above all apps or above GRID only. Its position is remembered, and **Dock Back** returns it to the deck.

## Privacy
- **New network request:** with a Now Playing widget following Spotify, GRID downloads the cover art from Spotify's own image server, without cookies. Apple Music artwork comes from the Music app itself.
- **Unchanged:** no accounts, telemetry or cloud services, and settings, history and browsing data stay on your Mac. The other requests GRID makes on its own are still the ad-blocking filter lists (when ad blocking is on) and Safari's standard fraudulent-site check.

## Known limitations
- Not notarized yet (see Install).
- **Now Playing covers Apple Music and Spotify only.** macOS 15.4 and later keep the system-wide now-playing information (audio in a browser, podcasts) for Apple's own apps.
- **Permission may be asked again after an update.** This build is signed ad hoc, so macOS can treat a new copy as a new app and ask for Automation permission again.
- **Custom widgets can't be popped out yet.** They run in their own sandboxed helper process tied to the deck.
- Text fields still show the standard Cut/Copy/Paste menu.
