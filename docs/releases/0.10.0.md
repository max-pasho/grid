**Pre-release.** GRID 0.10.0 adds **directions that avoid mapped licence-plate cameras**, worked out entirely on your Mac, with **print** and **GPX** export. Please try it and report problems. If you're on 0.9.0 or 0.8.0, replace GRID.app with this one; your settings, layout, widgets, browsing data and downloaded map regions carry over.

![The Map tilted over Homewood and Vestavia Hills: a route from A to B on the 3D terrain, with the Route panel comparing the camera-avoiding and fastest routes and the turn-by-turn steps](https://github.com/max-pasho/grid/blob/v0.10.0/docs/screenshots/directions.jpg?raw=true)

## Install
1. Download **GRID-0.10.0-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **GRID.app** to Applications (replace the old one if asked).
2. This build isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open it once, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (you only need to do this once).
3. Optional: check the download against **GRID-0.10.0-macOS.zip.sha256** with `shasum -a 256 GRID-0.10.0-macOS.zip`.

**Requires macOS 15** (browser extensions need macOS 15.4). Built for Apple silicon.

Because this is a pre-release, GRID's update checker offers it only if **Settings › Updates › Include pre-releases** is on.

## What's new since 0.9.0
- **Directions.** Right-click the map for **Directions from Here** / **Directions to Here**, use **Directions To / From** in a place's card, or the new directions button in the map's header.
  - **Two routes:** GRID shows the **fastest** route and one that **avoids the mapped ALPR cameras** it can, each with distance, time, the number of mapped cameras it passes and a risk level.
  - **Turn-by-turn steps:** turns, ramps, merges and roundabout exits, with each camera listed under the step where you pass it. Click a step to see it on the map.
  - **On the map:** the chosen route is drawn bright, the other dashed, with A and B marking the ends and the cameras on the route in orange. Routes follow the 3D terrain when tilted.
  - **Options:** set how close a road must pass a camera to count (25–150 m), and whether to use the direction cameras face.
- **Routes are worked out on your Mac.** The first route in an area offers to download that region's OpenStreetMap road data (Alabama is about 150 MB) and builds a road network from it in seconds. After that, directions work offline, and **your start, destination and routes are never sent anywhere**.
- **Print** a route: a clean black-on-white map of the whole trip with the route in blue, then the turn list in large type, over as many pages as needed.
- **GPX** for a GPS unit or an offline navigator: the start, destination and passed cameras as waypoints, the steps as a route and the full line as a track. **Copy** puts the turn list on the clipboard as text.
- **Fixed: the map's right-click menu closing itself.** A two-finger trackpad click could open the menu and close it again at once; it now stays open.
- **Smoother tilted views:**
  - **Camera markers** hide behind hills and buildings, and shrink and fade towards the horizon instead of piling up.
  - **The bottom bar:** in a narrow window, the coordinates move above the attribution instead of overlapping it.

## Privacy
- **Road data** comes from Geofabrik (**download.geofabrik.de**, already used for the region list), one region at a time.
  - It downloads only after you confirm, with its size shown first.
  - Geofabrik learns which region you chose.
  - The download is deleted once GRID has built its road network.
- **Routing itself makes no network requests.** Starts, destinations and routes stay in memory unless you print, save or copy them.
- **Clear Map Data** also removes road data, and Settings › Map lists and removes it by region.
- **Unchanged:** no accounts, telemetry or cloud services.

## Known limitations
- Not notarized yet (see Install).
- **Mapped cameras only:** routes avoid only cameras mapped in OpenStreetMap and downloaded to GRID. Coverage is incomplete and may be out of date, so a route can't be guaranteed unseen.
- **Some trips have no camera-free route.** GRID then shows the fewest it can and lists them.
- **One region at a time:** routes from one region's road data into another's (across a state line) aren't supported yet.
- **No traffic, closures or time-based restrictions.** Travel times are estimates.
- **Region size:** regions with road data over 700 MB (such as California or Texas) can't be built on the Mac yet.
