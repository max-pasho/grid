GRID 1.0.0 is the first full release with the complete **Map**. It adds place search, **directions that avoid mapped licence-plate cameras** worked out entirely on your Mac, and print and GPX export. It also includes a full review of the app for speed, security and polish. If you're on 0.8.0 or a pre-release, replace GRID.app with this one; your settings, layout, widgets, browsing data and downloaded map regions carry over.

![The Map tilted over Homewood and Vestavia Hills: a route from A to B on the 3D terrain, with the Route panel comparing the camera-avoiding and fastest routes and the turn-by-turn steps](https://github.com/max-pasho/grid/blob/v1.0.0/docs/screenshots/directions.jpg?raw=true)

## Install
1. Download **GRID-1.0.0-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **GRID.app** to Applications (replace the old one if asked).
2. This build isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open it once, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (you only need to do this once).
3. Optional: check the download against **GRID-1.0.0-macOS.zip.sha256** with `shasum -a 256 GRID-1.0.0-macOS.zip`.

**Requires macOS 15** (browser extensions need macOS 15.4). Built for Apple silicon.

## What's new since 0.8.0

### Directions that avoid mapped cameras
- **Starting a route:** right-click the map for **Directions from Here** / **Directions to Here**, use **Directions To / From** in a place's card, or the directions button in the map's header.
- **Two routes:** GRID shows the **fastest** route and one that **avoids the mapped ALPR cameras** it can. Each has distance, time, the number of mapped cameras it passes and a risk level.
- **Turn-by-turn steps:** turns, ramps, merges and roundabout exits, with each camera listed under the step where you pass it.
- **On the map:** the chosen route is bright, the other dashed, with A and B at the ends and the cameras on the route in orange. Routes follow the 3D terrain.
- **Worked out on your Mac:** the first route in an area offers to download that region's OpenStreetMap road data (Alabama is about 150 MB) and builds a road network from it in seconds. After that, directions work offline, and your start, destination and routes are never sent anywhere.
- **Print** a route: a clean black-on-white map with the route in blue, then the turn list in large type.
- **Export:** save the route as **GPX** for a GPS unit or offline navigator, or **Copy** the turn list as text.

### Places and search
- **Places:** shops, food, stops, sights, lodging and more show as small icons. Filter them by category in the **Places** layer, and click one for its card.
- **Search places** (⌘F on the Map page):
  - **Typing** searches the names in the map already on your Mac, with no network.
  - **Online search** runs only when you press Return with no match, press ⌘Return, or choose **Search online**. It asks the first time.

### Smoother and safer
- **Smooth city views:** zooming and panning over dense cities such as Manhattan is smooth again, at about 120 fps. Labels no longer stutter as place names appear, and map tiles appear without blocking the app.
- **Tilted views:**
  - **Camera markers** hide behind hills and buildings, and shrink and fade towards the horizon.
  - **Region outlines** stay drawn when you zoom past their corners.
- **Right-click menus** no longer close themselves after a two-finger trackpad click, anywhere in GRID.
- **Faster:** directions are about twice as fast, typing in the map's search box is far faster, and building road data uses less memory.
- **Downloads in the browser** are always quarantined by macOS, even if you close their tab mid-download. A download that fails no longer leaves a partial file behind.
- **Files:**
  - **Symlinks:** a symlink to a script now asks before running, and copy, move and Replace handle symlinked folders safely.
  - **Large folders** stay responsive while you filter.
- **Updates:** Download & Verify only follows GitHub's own download hosts.
- **Custom widgets** can't crash, hang or flood GRID, and can't target GRID's own processes.
- **Fixes:**
  - **Location error cards** can be dismissed.
  - **Shortcuts:** clearing a keyboard shortcut now leaves it unassigned. Before, it restored the default.

## Privacy
- **Road data** for directions comes from Geofabrik (**download.geofabrik.de**) one region at a time.
  - It downloads only after you confirm, with its size shown first.
  - Geofabrik learns which region you chose.
  - Routing itself makes no network requests.
- **Online place search** uses Nominatim (**nominatim.openstreetmap.org**, or a server you choose).
  - It runs only when you ask, never while you type, and asks before the first search.
  - At most one request a second.
  - Answers are kept in memory for up to a day; searches are never saved.
  - Turn it off in Settings › Map.

| Host | When | What it learns |
|---|---|---|
| tiles.openfreemap.org | While the map is visible | Roughly where you're looking |
| elevation-tiles-prod.s3.amazonaws.com | While the map is visible and Terrain is on | Roughly where you're looking |
| download.geofabrik.de | When you open the region list or download road data | That you opened it, which region's road data |
| deflock.dontgetflocked.com | When you download or check a US or Canadian camera region | Which region you downloaded |
| Your Overpass server | When you download a camera region elsewhere | Which area you downloaded |
| nominatim.openstreetmap.org (or your server) | When you search online | What you searched for and roughly where you're looking |

- **Unchanged:** no accounts, telemetry or cloud services. **Settings › Map › Clear Map Data** removes all map data, including road data.

## Known limitations
- Not notarized yet (see Install).
- **Mapped cameras only:** routes avoid only cameras mapped in OpenStreetMap and downloaded to GRID. Coverage is incomplete and may be out of date, so a route can't be guaranteed unseen. Some trips have no camera-free route.
- **One region at a time:** directions don't cross from one region's road data into another's. Regions over 700 MB of road data (such as California or Texas) can't be built yet.
- **No traffic, closures or time-based restrictions;** travel times are estimates.
- **Not included yet:** live traffic.
