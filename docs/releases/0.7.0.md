GRID 0.7.0 brings the **Map** to everyone. It's drawn in GRID's style, and when you zoom out it becomes a 3D globe you can spin. It shows the licence-plate reader (ALPR) cameras mapped by OpenStreetMap volunteers, downloaded for the regions you choose and kept offline. If you're on 0.5.x or the 0.6.0 pre-release, replace GRID.app with this one; your settings, layout, widgets, browsing data and downloaded map regions carry over.

![The Map zoomed out: a 3D globe with glowing coastlines, a latitude/longitude grid, country labels and camera clusters](https://github.com/max-pasho/grid/blob/v0.7.0/docs/screenshots/globe.png?raw=true)

## Install
1. Download **GRID-0.7.0-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **GRID.app** to Applications (replace the old one if asked).
2. This build isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open it once, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (you only need to do this once).
3. Optional: check the download against **GRID-0.7.0-macOS.zip.sha256** with `shasum -a 256 GRID-0.7.0-macOS.zip`.

**Requires macOS 15** (browser extensions need macOS 15.4). Built for Apple silicon.

## What's new since 0.6.0
- **A 3D globe when you zoom out.** The globe has a latitude/longitude grid, a glowing edge, and coastlines and borders as glowing lines. Drag to spin it.
- **A smooth zoom between globe and map.** As you zoom in, the globe unrolls into the flat map; nothing jumps. Land, water and the grid floor fade in as the globe fades out.
- **Tilt and turn with the trackpad:** hold **⌥** and swipe with two fingers. Up and down tilts the 3D view; left and right turns it.
- **Smoother edges.** Buildings, roads and outlines are anti-aliased. This uses no extra memory or CPU.

## The Map (new if you're coming from 0.5.x)
- **Opening it:** the Map is page **05** (⌥⌘5); Settings is now page **06**.
- **The look:** glowing roads, a faint grid floor, dark water, and wireframe buildings when you tilt. It follows your theme (Legacy, Ares, ENCOM) and Effects › Glow. It draws only when something changes, so it uses no CPU while you're not moving it.
- **ALPR cameras by region:** **Add Region…** lists US states, Canadian provinces and other countries' regions; **Draw Area** lets you ⇧-drag a rectangle on the map. Before anything downloads, GRID shows the host, the number of requests and the size. Downloaded regions stay on your Mac and work offline, and you can update, cancel or remove each one.
- **On the map:** each camera is a reticle with its facing direction. Zoomed out, cameras gather into counted clusters. Click one for its vendor, operator, facing, last edit and OpenStreetMap entry.
- **Locate:** **⌖ Locate** centres the map on you after macOS asks. Your position stays in memory only.
- **Freshness:** **Settings › Updates** shows how current each region is.

## Privacy
- **Off until you turn it on:** the Map makes no network requests until you click **Enable Map Data**. After that:

| Host | When | What it learns |
|---|---|---|
| tiles.openfreemap.org | While the map is visible | Roughly where you're looking |
| download.geofabrik.de | When you open the region list | That you opened it |
| deflock.dontgetflocked.com | When you download or check a US or Canadian region | Which region you downloaded |
| Your Overpass server | When you download a region elsewhere | Which area you downloaded |

- **Your location:** read only when you click Locate. It's never saved, logged or sent by GRID.
- **Unchanged:** no accounts, telemetry or cloud services. **Settings › Map › Clear Map Data** removes all map data.

## Known limitations
- Not notarized yet (see Install).
- **Camera data:** only as complete and current as OpenStreetMap's volunteers have made it.
- **The globe:** coastlines outline the land; there's no solid land fill.
- **The flat map:**
  - When tilted all the way, tiles at the far edge can occasionally be missing.
  - The cameras' facing wedges don't tilt with the ground.
  - There's no right-click menu on the map yet.
- **Downloads:** a rectangle drawn just outside the US uses the US source and finds nothing.
- **Locate:** not tested on a Developer-ID-signed build.
- **Not included yet:** search, traffic, routing and print export.
