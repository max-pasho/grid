GRID 0.8.0 gives the **Map** real terrain: shaded relief, contour lines with elevation labels, and 3D mountains and valleys when you tilt. Moving the map is also much smoother: labels stay locked to the map, and the tiles around you load before they scroll into view. If you're on 0.7.0, replace GRID.app with this one; your settings, layout, widgets, browsing data and downloaded map regions carry over.

![The Map tilted over the Alps near Zermatt: shaded 3D mountains with contour lines, elevation labels and town names](https://github.com/max-pasho/grid/blob/v0.8.0/docs/screenshots/terrain.jpg?raw=true)

## Install
1. Download **GRID-0.8.0-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **GRID.app** to Applications (replace the old one if asked).
2. This build isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open it once, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (you only need to do this once).
3. Optional: check the download against **GRID-0.8.0-macOS.zip.sha256** with `shasum -a 256 GRID-0.8.0-macOS.zip`.

**Requires macOS 15** (browser extensions need macOS 15.4). Built for Apple silicon.

## What's new since 0.7.0
- **Terrain.**
  - **Seen from above:** hills and mountains are shaded, with contour lines and elevation labels in feet or metres (following your Mac's region settings).
  - **Tilted:** the ground rises into real 3D mountains and valleys. Roads, buildings, cameras and labels sit on it.
  - **Height:** a little exaggerated by default so ranges stand out. Choose 1×, 1.5×, 2× or 3× in **Settings › Map**.
  - **Turning it off:** use the **Terrain** layer in the map's side column.
- **Smoother movement.**
  - **Labels** are drawn with the map in the same frame, so they no longer trail behind while you pan, zoom or turn.
  - **No empty ground:** a low-detail layer loads first, so you never see blank areas. Tiles around the view, and ahead of the direction you're moving, load before they come into view.
- **Steeper tilt:** up to 60°, with more detail up close and less in the distance.
- **Street names follow their roads** and read the right way up.
- **Right-click the map** to:
  - inspect a camera
  - centre the map
  - copy coordinates
  - open the spot in Apple Maps or OpenStreetMap
  - download the cameras in view
- **Camera wedges lie on the ground** and tilt with it.
- **The globe has solid land** under the oceans, not just coastlines.
- **Drawn areas just outside the US or Canada** (which the DeFlock index has nothing for) now download from your Overpass server instead of finding nothing.
- **Smoother lines:** thin roads and contours keep a clean, anti-aliased edge.

## Privacy
- **One new host:** elevation comes from the public AWS Terrain Tiles dataset. Like every map host, it's contacted only after you click **Enable Map Data**, and only while the Terrain layer is on. Elevation tiles are cached on your Mac (up to 150 MB).

| Host | When | What it learns |
|---|---|---|
| tiles.openfreemap.org | While the map is visible | Roughly where you're looking |
| elevation-tiles-prod.s3.amazonaws.com | While the map is visible and Terrain is on | Roughly where you're looking |
| download.geofabrik.de | When you open the region list | That you opened it |
| deflock.dontgetflocked.com | When you download or check a US or Canadian region | Which region you downloaded |
| Your Overpass server | When you download a region or drawn area outside the US and Canada | Which area you downloaded |

- **More tile requests:** to load ahead of you, the map now requests roughly 1.5–2.5 times as many tiles per view, all from the same hosts.
- **Unchanged:** no accounts, telemetry or cloud services. **Settings › Map › Clear Map Data** removes all map data, including cached elevation.

## Known limitations
- Not notarized yet (see Install).
- **Camera data:** only as complete and current as OpenStreetMap's volunteers have made it.
- **Elevation detail:** about 30 m. Small features like cliffs and quarries are smoothed out.
- **Very fast swipes:** a tile at the edge can still appear a moment late.
- **Memory:** the map can use a few hundred MB while you're exploring.
- **Locate:** not tested on a Developer-ID-signed build.
- **Not included yet:** search, traffic, routing and print export.
